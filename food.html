Korean Street Food's Biggest Export Is Finally Here

Tteokbokki — dense, chewy rice cakes in a spicy-sweet sauce — has surged on social media and is now showing up in grocery stores, cookbooks, and restaurants far outside Korea. The dish got a massive boost from BTS singer Jimin posting about his love of it, sending searches through the roof.

From sticky chili-honey glazed versions to fusion twists, tteokbokki is the food world's crossover moment of 2026. If you haven't tried it yet, that's about to change.
