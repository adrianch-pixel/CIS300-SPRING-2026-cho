Top Story — Sports Is the New Fashion Week

Tennis courts, basketball arenas, and football sidelines have officially replaced runways as the hottest stages in fashion. Last year, the US Open generated nearly 700% more online coverage than New York Fashion Week — and that gap is only growing in 2026.

From courtside fits to tunnel walk looks going viral before tip-off, athletes are now the most influential people in style. Brands are moving their budgets from front-row seats to locker room deals — and the results are reshaping what culture looks like.

About Vane

My website, Vane, is intended to be a culture and trends magazine site. The name comes from a weather vane — an instrument that shows which direction the wind is blowing — which felt fitting for a site focused on tracking where culture is headed. The website covers four main areas: People, Trends, Food, and Music. Each page features stories and coverage on what's happening right now across those spaces.
